*Roja* was A. R. Rahman's debut album, released in 1992. What's strange about it is that it hasn't
aged — put it on now and it still sounds refreshing and new, which is not something you can say
about most film soundtracks from thirty years ago.

I was genuinely curious about how someone creates something that mesmerising on a first attempt.
So I started digging: pulling the songs apart, working out what was actually going on inside them,
learning whatever I needed to learn to understand what I was hearing.

That's where the idea for this series came from. Episode 1 is *Chinni Chinni Aasa* — the song that
started the digging, and the reason there are any other episodes at all.
