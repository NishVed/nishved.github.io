**Decoding music, one song at a time.**

Most of us know far more music than we can explain. A song makes you feel something and the
reason stays just out of reach — it's in a time signature you didn't count, a chord that resolved
somewhere you didn't expect, two instruments answering each other in a gap between verses.

This series is about Indian film music: what it takes to create a song, how the parts work
together, and why the result sounds the way it does. It tells you more about something you've
already heard — a piece of music from a film or a composer you know well.

It's the same instinct as the [architecture work](/work/) — take something complex, find the
structure inside it, then explain it so the structure is obvious to someone else.
