I travel badly by most standards. I don't cover much ground, and I miss things I was supposed to
see.

What I do instead is eat where people actually eat, listen to what's playing, and stay somewhere
long enough that the second day is different from the first. The interesting part of a place is
rarely on the list — it's in what's normal there and isn't normal anywhere else.

Food and music turn out to be the fastest way in. Both tell you what a place values, what it had
to work with, and who passed through it.
