I play guitar, and I teach it.

Teaching is the part that changed how I hear music. You can play something correctly for years
without being able to say why it works. The moment a student asks *why does that chord sound
sad* — or worse, *why does mine sound wrong when I'm playing the same notes* — you have to
actually know the answer, and vague intuition stops being enough.

That pressure is where [Beyond Listening](/beyond-listening/) came from. Most of the questions in
that series are questions students asked first.

It's the same loop as architecture, honestly. You don't fully understand a system until you've
had to explain it to someone who will immediately find the part of your explanation that doesn't
hold up.
