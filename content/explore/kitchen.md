Cooking is the most immediate feedback loop I have access to.

A system you architect takes months to tell you whether you were right. A dish tells you in
twenty minutes, and it is completely unsentimental about it. You change one variable — heat,
timing, the order things go into the pan — and you find out.

What keeps me interested isn't recipes so much as technique: why an emulsion breaks, why resting
matters, why the same ingredients produce something entirely different depending on sequence.
Recipes are the interface. Technique is the implementation.
